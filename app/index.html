    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: play the story first, clear the named missions and stranger strands as they appear, then sweep activities and collectibles, and finish with the 100% cleanup. No mission chapter is guessed here — a stage names only what the official descriptions name.

      Launch-night context (Exophase, 638 tracked players, 19 Nov 2026): the opening trophy sat at 100%, the story chapters at about 1%, and the platinum at 0.63% — those early numbers came from reviewers and day-one players, and they fall as more players join tracking.

      Missables — earn these on your first pass

      GTA 6 has no confirmed mission replay at launch, so anything tied to a named mission or a one-time story moment should be done while it is in front of you. Each card below says exactly what can be missed and when.

        Next up — your five easiest wins

        The planner ranks your un-earned trophies by difficulty, then by roadmap order, so the next thing to chase is always the cheapest win. Earn one and the list re-ranks itself.
